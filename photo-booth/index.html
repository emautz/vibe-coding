The booth rules

1. Start the camera when you're ready.

2. Pick a filter. It applies to the preview and all four pictures.

3. Press Take 4 photos. A big countdown appears before every shot.

4. Your finished strip appears below. Save it as a PNG.

Tip: Find some good light, look toward the camera, and try a different pose for each shot.
