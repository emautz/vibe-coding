Iced Salted Maple Latte

Serves 1 • 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • Espresso
  • Maple syrup
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Ice
  • Water

Instructions

  1. Brew a double shot of espresso.
  2. In a cup, fill to the brim with ice.
  3. Pour in 215 ml of milk.
  4. Pour in 15 ml of maple syrup.
  5. Pour the double shot of espresso over the top.
  6. Add salt to taste.
  7. Stir vigorously and enjoy!
