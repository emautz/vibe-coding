How to explore

  • W or ↑ — Walk forward.
  • S or ↓ — Walk backward.
  • A or ← — Move left.
  • D or → — Move right.
  • Drag the mouse to look around.
  • Click a glowing stage to change its colors.

Tip: Click inside the 3D scene first so the keyboard controls can receive your movement keys.

World description

Welcome to a lively outdoor alternative music festival inspired by the energy of Vans Warped Tour. Under a bright blue sky, a warm sun shines over a wide, dusty field marked by patches of trampled grass. Tall palm trees frame the festival grounds, with their leafy crowns rising overhead.

Several stages stand across the venue, surrounded by speaker stacks, equipment, colorful signs, festival flags, vendor booths, and crowds of attendees in dark alternative clothing. The main stage glows with vivid colors, and each stage can be clicked to change its lighting. The atmosphere is sunny, busy, and full of summer concert energy.

This description is provided as text for visitors who cannot see or interact with the three-dimensional scene.
